# Interview Preparation: Features, Modes & Candidate Guidelines

## Overview
The Interview Preparation module empowers candidates to practice and excel in technical and behavioral interviews for specific internship roles. By analyzing the candidate's uploaded resume alongside the target internship's job description, the system produces tailored interview simulations, expected questions, model answer frameworks, and actionable feedback.

## Core Capabilities
- **Role-Specific Technical Questions**: Generates coding, system design, architectural, and conceptual questions corresponding to the required tech stack (e.g., Python, React, SQL, Machine Learning concepts).
- **Resume-Grounded Questions**: Formulates deep-dive inquiries into the specific projects, technologies, and achievements listed on the candidate's resume (e.g., "Explain how you handled data chunking in your RAG project").
- **Behavioral Questions via the STAR Method**: Formulates situational and competency questions structured to be answered using the **Situation, Task, Action, Result (STAR)** technique.
- **Answer Evaluation & Feedback**: Analyzes candidate practice responses, highlighting strengths, missing technical details, clarity of explanation, and tips for improvement.
- **Company & Culture Insights**: Provides overview questions tailored to company culture, leadership principles, and domain problems.

## Practice Modes Available

### 1. Self-Paced Question Bank Mode
- The system generates a curated list of 10 to 15 questions divided into three categories: *Technical Core*, *Project Deep-Dive*, and *Behavioral & Teamwork*.
- Users can view question intent, key evaluation criteria, and a structured outline of what an exemplary answer entails.

### 2. Interactive Mock Practice Mode
- The candidate selects a question and types or records their practice answer.
- The AI evaluates the answer against standard rubric metrics:
  - *Completeness*: Did the answer directly address all parts of the question?
  - *STAR Structure*: Did the behavioral response clearly articulate Situation, Task, Action, and quantifiable Result?
  - *Technical Depth*: Were appropriate technical principles, trade-offs, and terminology applied?
- The module provides constructive suggestions on how to improve the response.

## Step-by-Step Instructions: How to Use Interview Preparation
1. **Open an Active Application**: From your **Application Tracker** or **Internship Matches**, select the position for which you have an upcoming interview.
2. **Launch Interview Prep**: Click the **Prepare for Interview** button on the application record.
3. **Select Practice Focus**:
   - Choose between *Technical Focus*, *Behavioral Focus*, or *Comprehensive Simulation*.
   - Select difficulty level: *Foundational*, *Intermediate*, or *Advanced*.
4. **Generate Question Set**: Click **Generate Questions**. The system inspects your resume and the job posting to generate custom questions.
5. **Practice and Review**:
   - Work through each question sequentially.
   - Enter your response in the practice input area.
   - Click **Evaluate Answer** to receive immediate AI coaching, breakdown scores, and suggested enhancements.
6. **Save Notes & Review Later**: Bookmark challenging questions or save feedback to your interview notes for quick revision before the actual interview.

## Best Practices for Candidates
- **Utilize the STAR Method**: Always anchor behavioral responses in specific past scenarios rather than generic hypothetical answers.
- **Quantify Results**: Whenever discussing projects or past internships, cite concrete metrics (e.g., "reduced latency by 35%", "processed 10,000 records daily").
- **Acknowledge Limitations**: In technical questions, explaining trade-offs and acknowledging what you would research further is more effective than guessing.
