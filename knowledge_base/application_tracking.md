# Application Tracking: Dashboard, Statuses & Management Guide

## Overview
The Application Tracking module provides a centralized, organized workspace where candidates can monitor, manage, and update every internship application across its entire lifecycle. Instead of maintaining messy spreadsheets or losing track of deadlines across disparate email inboxes, candidates have full visibility into their recruitment pipeline.

## Application Lifecycle Stages
Each application moves through clearly defined workflow states:

1. **Wishlist / Saved**: Internships the candidate has bookmarked for future consideration but has not yet submitted.
2. **Applied**: Applications officially submitted to the employer.
3. **Under Review**: Employer has confirmed receipt and is screening candidate materials.
4. **Interview Scheduled**: Candidate has been invited for one or more interview rounds (HR screening, technical round, hiring manager interview).
5. **Offer Received**: Employer has formally extended an internship offer.
6. **Rejected**: Application was not advanced by the hiring organization.
7. **Withdrawn**: Candidate decided to withdraw their application (e.g., after accepting another offer).

## Tracked Data Points
For every internship application entry, the system tracks:
- **Company Name & Division**
- **Internship Role Title**
- **Job Location & Work Mode** (Remote / Hybrid / On-site)
- **Application Submission Date**
- **Application Deadline**
- **Current Status Stage**
- **Associated Cover Letter**: Direct link to the generated and customized cover letter file.
- **Matched Skill Score**: The original semantic match score at time of application.
- **Interview Dates & Links**: Calendar dates, times, and video conference links (Zoom, Google Meet, Teams).
- **Personal Notes & Contacts**: Recruiter names, emails, follow-up dates, and interview feedback notes.

## Step-by-Step Instructions: How to Track Your Applications
1. **Access the Tracker**: Click **Application Tracker** in the main navigation menu.
2. **View Your Pipeline**:
   - **Kanban Board View**: Displays applications as draggable cards across status columns (*Saved*, *Applied*, *Reviewing*, *Interviewing*, *Offered*, *Rejected*).
   - **Table / List View**: Displays a sortable tabular view filterable by submission date, company name, or deadline.
3. **Add an Application**:
   - *Automatic Addition*: Selecting an internship from the **Internship Matching** module and clicking *Add to Pipeline* automatically creates an entry.
   - *Manual Addition*: Click **+ Add External Application** to record an internship found outside the platform (enter company, role, link, and date).
4. **Update Application Status**:
   - Drag a card from one column to another on the Kanban board, OR
   - Open the application detail modal and select a new status from the dropdown menu.
5. **Log Notes & Interviews**:
   - When an interview is scheduled, open the application card and input the date, time, interviewer name, and round type.
   - Use the **Notes** section to log discussion points, questions asked, and follow-up deadlines.
6. **Filter & Search**:
   - Use the search bar to locate specific companies or roles.
   - Filter by status, work arrangement, or date range.
