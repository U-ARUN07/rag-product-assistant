# Cover Letter Generation: Features, Options & User Instructions

## Overview
The Cover Letter Generation feature uses Large Language Models (LLMs) to craft highly personalized, professional, and targeted cover letters for specific internship applications. Generic cover letters are notoriously ineffective; this module dynamically aligns the candidate's actual projects, coursework, and technical skills with the exact requirements and values of the target hiring company.

## Core Capabilities
- **Candidate-Specific Customization**: Pulls directly from the candidate's verified profile, highlighting relevant projects, technologies, and achievements that match the selected role.
- **Job-Specific Alignment**: Analyzes the target employer's job description to mirror key terminology, mission, and required qualifications.
- **Dynamic Tone Selection**: Allows the user to choose an appropriate voice (e.g., Professional/Formal, Conversational/Enthusiastic, Technical/Direct).
- **Project Highlight Selector**: Enables candidates to explicitly specify which 1 or 2 past projects should be emphasized in the letter body.
- **Editable Inline Interface**: Provides a full rich-text editor so the candidate can inspect, refine, tweak, or regenerate sections before exporting.

## Inputs Used for Cover Letter Synthesis
1. **Candidate Profile**:
   - Full name and contact information.
   - Educational background and major.
   - Specific skills matching the position.
   - Top project achievements and quantifiable results.
2. **Target Role Data**:
   - Hiring company name.
   - Job title / Internship position.
   - Stated responsibilities and technical requirements.
   - Team or division focus.
3. **User-Configured Parameters**:
   - Chosen tone of voice.
   - Target length (Standard ~300-400 words, Concise ~200-250 words).
   - Additional personal notes or focal points.

## Step-by-Step Instructions: How to Generate a Cover Letter
1. **Select an Internship**: From your **Internship Matches** or **Saved Pipeline**, click on the position you are applying for.
2. **Click "Generate Cover Letter"**: On the internship details card, click the **Generate Cover Letter** button.
3. **Configure Generation Settings**:
   - Select your preferred tone: *Professional*, *Enthusiastic*, or *Academic*.
   - Check the specific projects or skills you want highlighted in the body paragraphs.
   - (Optional) Enter custom notes, such as why this particular company inspires you.
4. **Click "Generate"**: The system prompts the LLM with your profile context and job details to synthesize the draft within 3–5 seconds.
5. **Review and Polish**: Read through the generated draft. The platform provides an interactive editor to adjust phrasing, add personal anecdotes, or make stylistic changes.
6. **Export and Save**:
   - Click **Save to Application** to attach the cover letter to your application tracking record.
   - Click **Download PDF** or **Download Word (.docx)** to obtain a clean, professionally formatted file ready for submission.

## Best Practices and Realistic Limitations
- **Mandatory Human Review**: Candidates must always review the generated cover letter before submitting. While the LLM is instructed not to fabricate facts, candidates must verify all dates, metrics, and personal narratives.
- **Avoid Over-Automation**: A cover letter is most effective when it includes genuine human intent. Add a sentence about personal motivation or connection to the company.
- **No Direct Portal Submission**: The platform generates and exports the cover letter document for you; it does not automatically submit job applications on third-party employer websites.
